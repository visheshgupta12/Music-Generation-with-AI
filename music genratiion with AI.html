♪ MelodyMind

Teach an AI to compose music.

MelodyMind learns from MIDI songs, trains an LSTM neural network right in your browser, and then writes brand new melodies that you can play, download as MIDI, or save as audio.

Or go step by step below. Training takes about a minute.

Collect MIDI data

Seven classic folk melodies are built in. Click a song to include or exclude it, or upload your own .mid files.

Preprocess the data

Extract the melody, snap it to a 16th-note grid, turn it into tokens, add transposed copies, and cut it into training windows.

Tokens: · rest   – hold   note name = new note

Build the deep learning model

An Embedding layer, two LSTM layers and a softmax output that predicts the next musical step.

Train the model

The model reads 32 steps of music and learns to guess the next one. Loss goes down as it learns.

Generate new music

The trained model starts from a short seed and keeps predicting the next step to compose a new melody.
